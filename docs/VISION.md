# Vision

## One sentence

A cosy, slightly chaotic first-person tidy-up game where you and up to five mates clean a Swedish lake island after a night you barely remember — and the canoes won't leave until the camp leader is satisfied.

## The fantasy

You wake up with a headache in a tent that has half collapsed. Cans in the bushes, a paddle in a tree, someone's sock on the fire pit. Sunlight on the water. There is a *right place* for everything: the pant bag, the cooler, the dry bag, the tent bag with its poles in order, the canoe. Finding where each thing goes — and discovering that all six Tuborg cans should end up together — is the game. When the last item clicks into place the island goes quiet, the water sparkles, and the camp leader (who you never see) delivers a verdict.

## Pillars

1. **Satisfying sorting.** Picking up, recognising, and placing must feel great: crisp feedback for correct (gold flash, chime), gentle feedback for wrong (dull red, a shrug), a rising glow and a fanfare when a container is complete. Sound and music intensify as the island gets cleaner.
2. **Discovery, not instructions.** No checklist. You learn the rules by trying. Abilities you unlock (Insight, Call a mate, Map sense) are the game meeting you halfway.
3. **Made for a link.** The whole game loads in a browser tab from a URL. No installers. A friend on a laptop can join in under a minute.
4. **Better together.** Six people on one island, shouting "who has the fourth tent pole?" is the intended experience. The single-player game is the same game with quieter company.
5. **Honest scope.** One island done beautifully beats three done badly. Content is data (JSON), so more islands and items are cheap once the systems are right.

## Reference

*Librarian: Tidy Up the Arcane Library!* (ArtRising, 2026): first-person, 3,072 books, 400 shelf rows, three-layer placement rule (section → series → volume order), skill points from completed rows unlocking Assemble/Insight/Auto-Shelving, hidden Principal's Evaluation scoring accuracy and speed, exit barrier that opens only when everything is shelved. Single-player only — our multiplayer is the twist.

## Tone

Warm, Scandinavian-summer, mildly self-deprecating. Danish first (`da`), English available. Beer brands and snaps are part of the joke but the game never glorifies drinking — the hangover is the punchline.

## Success criteria

- A first-time player understands what to do within 60 seconds without reading anything.
- A full clean of Island 01 takes 15–25 minutes solo, 6–10 minutes with four people.
- Six friends can finish a session on GitHub Pages with no server we operate.
- The codebase stays approachable for an experienced developer who is new to game dev: every rule is a pure function with a test.

## Non-goals (for now)

Realistic physics simulation, competitive modes, mobile touch controls, voice chat, procedurally generated islands, monetisation.
